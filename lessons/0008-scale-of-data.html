Lesson 0008

How much data is "huge"?

"Trained on a huge pile of text" is the kind of phrase that sounds precise and means nothing. Before Lesson 0009 asks what huge scale does, this lesson asks how huge it actually is.

Guess before you look

GPT-3 (2020) was trained on roughly 1 trillion words of text — about 300–500 billion tokens, depending how you count. Reading at a typical adult's silent reading speed (about 250 words per minute), 8 hours a day, every single day with no days off — how long would it take one person to read that much?

And it kept growing

That was 2020. By 2024, Meta's Llama 3 was trained on roughly 15 trillion tokens — 7× more than its own predecessor, Llama 2, and on the order of 30–50× GPT-3's token count, just four years later. The "huge pile of text" a lesson casually gestures at isn't a fixed size. It's a number that has kept climbing by an order of magnitude every few years.
